Kids Homework

Privacy policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Kids Homework ("the app") is a personal, non-commercial tool that I, a parent, run on my own computer to keep track of my own children's school assignments. This policy explains what data the app accesses, how it is used, and how it can be deleted.

Who uses the app

The app is used only by me, for my own children's Google accounts, which are supervised through Google Family Link. Each child's account is connected only after I sign in with it and approve access as the parent. The app is not offered to anyone else and has no public sign-up.

What Google data the app accesses

With read-only permission, the app accesses:

  • The names of the child's active Google Classroom classes.
  • The child's assignments and class materials: titles, descriptions, due dates, links and attached files.
  • The files teachers attached to those assignments in Google Drive, in order to download a copy.
  • The child's email address, only to confirm that the correct account was connected.

The app does not access the child's email, contacts, grades, submissions, or any Drive files other than those attached to their classwork.

How the data is used

  • Attached files are saved to a folder on my own computer, organised by child and subject.
  • A short summary of each new assignment (title, due date, pages, file names and link) is added to a Google Sheet in my own Google account.
  • A record of which assignments have already been seen is kept on my computer, so nothing is processed twice.

The data is used only to show me my children's homework. It is not used for advertising, profiling, analytics, or training any AI or machine-learning model.

Sharing

The data is not sold, rented or shared with any third party. It is not transferred anywhere except my own computer and my own Google Sheet.

Limited Use

Kids Homework's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and security

  • Sign-in tokens are stored only on my computer, in files readable only by my user account. No passwords for Google accounts are stored by the app.
  • Downloaded files and the list of seen assignments stay on my computer.
  • Data is sent only over encrypted connections to Google's services.

Keeping and deleting data

  • Files and sheet rows are kept until I delete them.
  • Access can be removed at any time from the child's Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or by me as the parent in Google Family Link. Removing access stops the app immediately.
  • Deleting the app's sign-in tokens, downloaded files and record file from my computer removes all data the app holds.

Children's privacy

The app accesses children's school data only for the parent of those children, with that parent's approval through Google Family Link, and only for the purpose described above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
