Kids Homework

One place for my kids' homework

A small private tool I run on my own computer so I don't miss my children's assignments and due dates.

What it does

Twice a day, Kids Homework checks my children's school accounts and gathers their assignments in one place, so I can see what is due without relying on the children to check every site themselves.

  • Reads my children's classes and assignments from Google Classroom (read-only).
  • Downloads the files teachers attach to those assignments, so they are saved on my computer, organised by child and subject.
  • Adds one line per new assignment (title, due date, pages, file name, link) to a Google Sheet that belongs to me.
  • Sends me a notification on my computer when something new appears.
Who it is for. This is a personal, non-commercial tool used only by me, as a parent, for my own children's accounts, with their accounts supervised through Google Family Link. It is not offered to the public and has no sign-up.

What it never does

  • It never changes, submits, deletes or posts anything in Google Classroom or Google Drive. Access is read-only.
  • It never sells, shares or sends my children's data to anyone else, and shows no ads.
  • It does not use the data for anything other than showing me my children's homework.

Google permissions it asks for

  • classroom.courses.readonly: see the names of the child's classes.
  • classroom.coursework.me.readonly: see the child's own assignments and due dates.
  • classroom.courseworkmaterials.readonly: see class materials posted by teachers.
  • drive.readonly: download files that teachers attached to those assignments.

Details on how this data is handled are in the privacy policy.
